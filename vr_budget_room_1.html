VR Budget Room
Every box on the shelf is a monthly expense. Sort them into Needs, Wants, and Savings without going over your take-home pay. Target: 50 / 30 / 20. Pay for every need once. Some needs have a cheaper box (a roommate, a used car). Sweep moves the money that is left over into savings.
Desktop: drag to look, WASD to move, click a box to pick it up, click a bin to drop it. Click the shelf to put it back.
Quest: tap "VR", point a controller and pull the trigger.
